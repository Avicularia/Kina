# Kina

A gold companion for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

- **Hot Farms, live:** the mobs and gathering nodes paying far above their level right now, with a plain answer to "why": worth per kill, gold per hour at your pace, levelling fit, market depth and confidence.
- **Gold per hour, live:** every item at its best price, with AH prices shared by every Kina on the realm.
- **Prices, anywhere:** browse the auction house from anywhere in the world, from your last scan and other Kinas'.
- **Farm with friends:** everyone sees the same totals and the fair split. No debts, nothing to chase.
- **Season 1:** earn Renown and Kina XP by farming with other Kinas, with streaks, commendations and cosmetics.

**Status: released** on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1793386).

## Install
1. Download the latest `Kina-x.y.z.zip` from [Releases](../../releases).
2. Unzip into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` (you should get `Kina` and `KinaScoutC` folders).
3. In game: `/kina` opens the window, `/kina selftest` checks everything works.

Or with **WowUp**: *Install from URL* and paste this repository's link; updates arrive automatically.

Free, no ads, nothing for sale.
